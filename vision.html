THE VISION

To fill every campus with the knowledge of His glory.

Our vision is a Kenya where no student completes their degree without encountering the weight of God’s presence. We see a landscape of altars—where spiritual fervor is more contagious than campus culture.

  • 100% Institution Coverage
  • 24/7 National Prayer Chain
THE MISSION

Mobilizing altars that burn with the fire of consecration.

Our mission is to establish, resource, and sustain campus-wide prayer movements that focus on intimacy, intercession, and holiness. We disciple students to become carriers of God's presence in their lecture halls and dormitories.

  • Apostolic Discipleship
  • Holy Spirit Centered Gatherings

The Four Pillars of Zeal

The spiritual DNA that defines a UCPM Member.

Undivided Devotion

Intimacy is our first priority. We foster a generation that loves the Person of Jesus more than the performance of ministry.

High Consecration

Holiness is not a rule; it’s a posture of love. We raise students who choose to be set apart for the Master's use.

Intercessory Burden

We don't pray because we have to; we pray because we can't bear to see our campus remain the same without God.

Presence Carriers

The zeal of God doesn't stay in the prayer room—it follows the student into the lab, the library, and the marketplace.

Roadmap to
National Revival

UCPM follows a strategic spiritual roadmap designed to move beyond seasonal "rallies" toward a sustained national fire.

"The fire on the altar must be kept burning; it must never go out." — Leviticus 6:13

2024 - 2025: Ignition & Unity

Mobilizing regional prayer hubs and uniting the disparate campus altars into one cohesive national network. Standardizing the 'Altar Protocol' across 50 institutions.

2026 - 2027: Deep Consecration

Launching the National School of Intercession. Focusing on the "Daniel Generation"—students who can stand in Babylon without losing their spiritual fire or integrity.

2028 - 2029: Marketplace Flooding

Graduating thousands of "Zeal Carriers" who don't just look for jobs but see their professions as a continuation of the campus altar.

2030: Total Transformation

Reaching the point where every institution in Kenya has a self-sustaining UCPM altar, and campus unrest is replaced by campus revival.
