What is a UCPM Chapter?

We don't just register names; we establish spiritual standards. Every registered chapter must adhere to our "Altar Excellence" framework.

1. Spiritual Covering

A chapter must be officially recognized by the resident Christian Union (CU) and have at least one staff patron.

2. Prayer Rhythm

A minimum of 10 hours of collective intercession per week, focusing on national and institutional issues.

3. Accountability

Submission of monthly spiritual reports to the Regional Coordinator and the National Secretariat.

4. Kingdom Impact

Active participation in campus ethics, mentorship programs, and Student Council integrity drives.
