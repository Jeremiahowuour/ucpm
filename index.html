The Movement Vision

Uniting the Body of Christ in the Academy.

The Universities and Colleges Christian Union Prayer Movement (UCPM) is an interdenominational intercessory engine designed to bring spiritual stability to campuses.

Spiritual Awakening

Igniting a passion for the presence of God in every student union.

Divine Mentorship

Connecting student leaders with seasoned alumni and spiritual fathers.

"We are the generation that will not only graduate with certificates but with a mantle of leadership that changes nations." — UCPM National Chairperson

Our Four Pillars of Strength

Intercession

Establishing 24-hour prayer chains in institutions to stand in the gap for students and the national education sector.

Radical Unity

UCPM brings together various Christian fellowships, breaking denominational silos for a united campus front.

Integrity

Promoting holiness and ethical leadership in Student Representative Councils (SRCs) and Christian Union boards.

Transformation

Equipping students to influence the marketplace with biblical values immediately after their graduation.

A Coordinated National Network

UCPM is structured for accountability and scalability. We operate across 12 spiritual zones to ensure every institution receives coverage.
