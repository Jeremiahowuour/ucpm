Legal Information

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Privacy Policy

At UCPM, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the data you provide when registering as a member.

  • Data Collection: We collect your name, institution, and contact details solely for chapter management and communication.
  • Data Usage: Your data is used to verify institutional affiliation and to send you updates about national rallies.
  • Protection: We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or external organizations.

2. Terms of Membership

By registering as a member of UCPM, you agree to the following code of conduct:

  • Integrity: Members are expected to maintain high ethical standards on campus, especially if serving in student leadership.
  • Non-Discrimination: UCPM is an inclusive movement. We do not tolerate harassment or discrimination based on denomination or background.
  • Institution Rules: All UCPM chapters must operate within the legal frameworks and rules provided by their respective university administrations.

3. Media Release

By attending UCPM national rallies, you acknowledge that photographs and videos may be taken for promotional and impact reporting purposes. If you wish for your image not to be used, please contact the National Secretariat.
