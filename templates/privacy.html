Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data across our movement.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register as a student member, submit a chapter charter, or sign up for our newsletter, we collect personal information such as your full name, email address, WhatsApp number, institution name, and admission number.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for official administrative purposes within the Universities and Colleges Christian Union Prayer Movement. This includes issuing digital membership credentials, coordinating regional prayer networks, and communicating official announcements.

3. Data Security

We implement secure database protocols and restrict access to member information exclusively to authorized national secretariat administrators. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside organizations.

4. Communications

By registering or subscribing, you consent to receive periodic spiritual resources, event invitations, and movement updates. You can opt out of newsletter broadcasts at any time.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or wish to review your data records, reach out directly to our secretariat at info@ucpm-movement.org.
