Our Journey of Faith

The Universities and Colleges Christian Union Prayer Movement is more than an organization; it is a spiritual lineage dedicated to keeping the fire of the altar burning.

The Genesis

From a Dorm Room to a National Force

The UCPM story began in the late 2010s, amidst a season of great spiritual transition in our higher learning institutions. A small group of Christian Union leaders noticed a growing gap between academic excellence and spiritual fervor.

What started as a Wednesday Night Watch in a cramped hostel room soon became a regional cry. The burden was simple: "Lord, do not let our campuses become graveyards of faith, but nurseries of revival."

By 2020, even amidst global shifts, the movement formalized its structure, creating a bridge between various denominations and institutions. Today, we stand as the primary intercessory covering for over 50 campus chapters nationwide.

"UCPM was not built by men’s hands, but by the knees of students who refused to let their institutions go to the enemy."

Founding National Chairperson

The Pillars of Our Character

These are the non-negotiable values that define every UCPM member.

Spiritual Fervor

We maintain a high temperature of prayer. We believe that spiritual lukewarmness is the greatest threat to a student's destiny.

Biblical Integrity

Our leadership is rooted in the Word. We train students to be incorruptible leaders who honor God in their exams and offices.

Kingdom Unity

We celebrate diversity but demand unity. UCPM is a neutral ground where all denominations pray under one name: Jesus Christ.

Academic Excellence

Daniel was 10 times better. We believe a praying student must also be a studying student to represent the Kingdom in the marketplace.

Servant Leadership

Positions in UCPM are for service, not status. We follow the example of Christ who washed the feet of His disciples.

National Burden

Our prayers are not selfish. We intercede for the government, the economy, and the peace of our nation daily.

Our Theological Foundation

We stand on the historic Christian faith, emphasizing the power of the Holy Spirit in the academic environment.

The Holy Scriptures

The Triune God

The Power of Prayer

We believe that fervent, effectual prayer changes circumstances and aligns the earth with the will of Heaven.

Vision 2030 Roadmap

We are not just praying for today; we are building for the next decade. Our strategic plan ensures that the movement remains relevant and impactful.

  • PHASE 1
    Total Coverage (2026-2027)

    Ensuring every registered TVET, College, and University in the nation has a UCPM prayer altar.

  • PHASE 2
    Institutional Leadership (2028-2029)

    Placing UCPM-trained leaders in at least 60% of student governing bodies nationwide.

  • PHASE 3
    Global Student Altar (2030)

    Connecting with student prayer movements across 50 countries for a global campus watch.
