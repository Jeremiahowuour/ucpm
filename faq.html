No. UCPM is a non-denominational inter-university prayer movement. We do not hold Sunday services or replace the local church. We exist to mobilize students from all Christian backgrounds to pray for their institutions and the nation.

We work in partnership with existing Campus Christian Unions. UCPM acts as a specialized "prayer and leadership arm" that strengthens the CU’s intercessory capacity. We require all UCPM members to be active participants in their local CU.

Membership in UCPM is free. We believe in the free mobilization of students. However, certain national rallies or leadership conferences may have a registration fee to cover food, accommodation, and materials.

The process is simple:
  1. Identify at least 10 committed students.
  2. Obtain a recommendation letter from your CU Patron or Chairperson.
  3. Fill out the Chapter Registration form on our Membership page.
  4. Wait for a regional coordinator to visit for an induction session.

Daniel Governance is our flagship leadership curriculum. It is designed to train Christian students on how to run for campus student council seats and professional positions with high ethical standards and spiritual integrity, modeled after the biblical Daniel.
